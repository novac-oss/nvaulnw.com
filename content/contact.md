## Contact Form
Please send us the following. The more concrete you can be, the more useful our first reply will be.

**Fields:**

1. **Your name** — first and last.
2. **Company** — your studio, startup, or "independent" if you are solo.
3. **Email** — where we should reply.
4. **Project type** — choose one or more from:
   - Custom mobile app (iOS / Android / cross-platform)
   - Web or SaaS build
   - UI/UX or brand design
   - Mobile management app suite (our product line)
   - Game publishing on Google Play or App Store
   - Utility app publishing
   - Ad monetization or mediation setup
   - ASO and store-listing work
   - Analytics and growth
   - Other (tell us in the message)
5. **Message** — 2–6 sentences on the product, the audience, the deadline, and any tech, store or compliance constraints.

**What happens next:**
- We reply within 1 business day with a short acknowledgement.
- Within 3 business days we send a discovery brief — a few targeted questions so we can scope accurately.
- Within 5 business days we send a written proposal with team, timeline, milestones and price.

There is no charge for the discovery conversation and no obligation to proceed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1. What size of project do you take on?**
Anything from a 4-week MVP to multi-year platform engagements. Our sweet spot is a 3–6 month build of a single mobile product, or a longer-term publishing portfolio.

**2. Do you sign NDAs?**
Yes. We are happy to sign your mutual NDA before we receive any confidential information, and we can also send our standard one-way NDA for inbound enquiries.

**3. Do you work on a fixed-price or time-and-materials basis?**
Both, depending on what the project needs. Fixed-price works for clearly scoped MVPs and well-defined features. Time-and-materials works for ongoing product development, ad operations and live-ops work.

**4. Can you take over a half-built app?**
Yes. We do code and architecture audits, and we are used to stepping into codebases built in Swift, Kotlin, React Native, Flutter and Unity. We will give you a clear, honest read on what is salvageable and what needs to be rebuilt.

**5. How do you handle IP?**
On custom builds, all IP created for your project is assigned to you on final payment, including source code, designs and documentation. We retain the right to use anonymized techniques and patterns in our portfolio.

**6. Do you publish your own apps too?**
Yes. We run a catalogue of casual games and utility tools on Google Play and the App Store. This keeps our team sharp on store policy and ASO, and it means the same engineers who ship your app also ship our own.

**7. Can you help with App Store or Google Play rejections?**
Yes. We handle appeal letters, guideline-mapping, metadata fixes and re-submissions. We can also do a pre-submission audit on an app you already have in flight.
